## What's next: the series outline

Here are the upcoming steps. Each will have one or more posts, with costs, timelines and documents.

1. **Check the name in the Ministry of Commerce register.** Make sure "Chantrea Systems" is available, on the [Ministry of Commerce's business registration portal](https://www.businessregistration.moc.gov.kh/).
2. **Buy the domain names.**
3. **Find premises or a registered address service**, with a lease for the registered address. Current lead: a bungalow on a long-term lease where I'm staying.
4. **Gather the documents:** passport, photos, lease, articles of incorporation in Khmer and English.
5. **Choose the agent and the accountant.**
6. **Set up the company.**
7. **Apply for the work permit**, before the visa expires in March.

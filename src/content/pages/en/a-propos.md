I'm Stefan. I'm an expat living in Kampot, Cambodia, and I'm setting up my IT services company, **Chantrea Systems** ("chantrea" means "moonlight" in Khmer).

I have never set up a company in Cambodia. This blog tells how I go about it, step by step: what it costs, how long it takes, which documents are required, and the mistakes I make along the way.

Most posts are written in French first. Only posts that have been translated appear on the English side of the site.
